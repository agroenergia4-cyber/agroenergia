Who We Are

Promoting access to renewable energy in Mozambique

Agro Energia Moçambique is a pioneering startup committed to expanding sustainable renewable energy access in rural Mozambique.

Our mission is to empower communities through access to clean, reliable, and affordable energy technologies that improve quality of life, enhance agricultural productivity, and promote environmental sustainability.

Long-term environmental and economic impact

Our Vision

To be the leading provider of renewable energy solutions in Mozambique, enabling every rural community to access clean, sustainable energy for cooking, lighting, and productive activities.

Our Solutions

Renewable Energy Technologies

Biogas Technologies

We transform organic waste into clean cooking gas and electricity, helping reduce deforestation, improve public health, and turn agricultural residues into valuable energy.

  • Domestic biogas digesters
  • Community-scale systems
  • Biofertiliser production

Solar Energy Systems

Harness the power of the sun with our range of solar solutions, from pico-solar lighting to complete home and agricultural systems.

  • Pico solar home systems
  • Solar dryers for agriculture
  • Solar cooking products

Energy Management Software

Advanced software solutions to monitor, optimize, and manage renewable energy infrastructure for maximum efficiency and reliability.

  • Real-time monitoring
  • Performance analytics
  • Predictive maintenance
