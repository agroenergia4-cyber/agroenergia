Who We Are

Promoting access to renewable energy in Mozambique

Agro Energia Moçambique is a pioneering startup committed to expanding sustainable renewable energy access in rural Mozambique.

Our mission is to empower communities through access to clean, reliable, and affordable energy technologies that improve quality of life, enhance agricultural productivity, and promote environmental sustainability.

Our Vision

To be the leading provider of renewable energy solutions in Mozambique, enabling every rural community to access clean, sustainable energy for cooking, lighting, and productive activities.

Our Projects

Driving Impact Through Innovation

Featured Project

Green Poultry Farm Mozambique

Green Poultry Farm Mozambique is a social project that converts poultry manure into biogas and biofertilisers, promoting a circular economy and providing sustainable energy solutions for smallholder poultry farmers in rural Mozambique.

Energy Source: Poultry manure & agro waste
Capacity so far: 13,000 cubic metres of biogas
Impact: Waste management & clean energy
