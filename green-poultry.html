Project Overview

Transforming Poultry Farming into a Zero-Waste, Energy-Positive System

The Green Poultry Farm is an innovative demonstration project that integrates modern poultry production with biogas technology and sustainable waste management. Located in Mozambique, this facility showcases how agricultural operations can become self-sufficient in energy while contributing to environmental conservation.

By converting poultry waste into biogas for cooking and electricity generation, and bio-slurry for organic fertilizer, the farm operates as a circular economy model that maximizes resource efficiency while minimizing environmental impact.

Technology & Innovation

Integrated Biogas & Renewable Energy Systems

1

Waste Collection

Daily collection of poultry manure and organic waste from 5,000 birds, providing consistent feedstock for biogas production.

→
2

Anaerobic Digestion

Waste is processed in a 50m³ biogas digester where microorganisms break down organic matter, producing methane-rich biogas.

→
3

Energy Generation

Biogas powers cooking facilities, heaters for chick brooding, and generators for electricity, making the farm energy self-sufficient.

→
4

Bio-Fertilizer Production

Nutrient-rich bio-slurry from the digester is processed into organic fertilizer, sold to local farmers for crop production.

50m³ Digester

Industrial-grade fixed dome biogas digester with automated feeding system

Solar Backup

5kW solar PV system provides supplementary power and ensures energy reliability

Smart Monitoring

IoT sensors track gas production, temperature, and system performance in real-time
